Mission

Our mission is to showcase the fun side of maths, and to make maths accessible to local students who may be considering higher education in STEM.

The OMTC was created by Oxford students from all over the world to bring something new to the British maths culture: an exhibition of challenging maths, free to attend, made for students by students. With emphasis on access and state-school participation, we hope to show our appreciation for maths as collaborative and entertaining, and to inspire participants to study a mathematical field in the future.
